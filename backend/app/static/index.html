Privacy & Local Data Management

This application operates under a strict local-first architecture. Data is stored in your local SQLite database (`mood_journal.db`) and processed directly by your laptop's Python runtime.

  • No Cloud Tracking: No external analytics, telemetry, or remote LLM APIs.
  • Complete Portability: Export all entries, sentiment scores, emotion classifications, and corrections as CSV.
  • Right to Deletion: Permanently wipe all stored journal entries in a single action.

Personalization & Adaptation Controls

The emotion classifier incorporates Empirical Bayes Prior Adjustment with Bounded Shrinkage. It uses your confirmed and corrected emotions to calibrate predictions to your personal emotional vocabulary.
